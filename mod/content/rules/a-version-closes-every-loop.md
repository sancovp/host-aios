# A BRIDGE STANDS IN FOR A PART THAT MUST STAY OFF, SO THAT WHAT REQUIRES IT CAN BE TESTED

TRIGGER:[a thing is to be tested or proven, and a part it requires must stay off for now — not built, not ready, or
HELD · about to call something a bridge, a stand-in, a mock or a workaround · a part found off, away or switched off ·
about to move a thing's work elsewhere, redesign it or ask about it because the part it runs on is off · a goal's
table written for a version that has a bridge · the part a bridge stands in for coming on]

PRINCIPLE:[a bridge exists for the sake of the thing being tested, never for the part that is off · it is a crude
version of exactly the one step the missing part does — the same result, without that part's way of reaching it — so
the mechanism is seen working end to end · the real part comes on later, and the bridge goes then · a part that is
merely off for now — switched off, paused, waiting its turn — needs no bridge and changes no design: it is turned on
when it is wanted, by whoever turned it off · a part MUST STAY OFF only where the design or *USER* says it does (not
built, not ready, HELD for a later version); every other part found off is merely off for now, a part *USER* switched
off among them]

DO:[before making a bridge, name three things: what is being tested · the part it requires that must stay off, and why
it must · the one step of that part the bridge does — and make the bridge only when all three are named · make it small
and plain: that one step as a plain call, simulated or mocked · write it into the design, and give it its row on the
board and in the goal's table · prove it by watching the tested thing work end to end through it · say plainly what the
bridge does not do that the real part will · remove it in the same change that brings the part on; its row on the board stays, at its last state · list a loop found
stopping at a part that is off as stopped, with the part it waits for; it gets a bridge only when the three are named,
and otherwise it waits for its part]

COMPOSES:[[[close-the-failure-class]] — a tested thing that cannot be seen working is a failure class, and the bridge is its guard · [[00-the-work-loop]] — a goal carries the next planned release version whole, its bridges among its deliverables · [[22-seq-write-a-goal]] — each bridge a row of the goal's table]

NOT:[make a bridge because something is off, away or switched off · route around a thing that is off for now — move its
work to another place, or redesign for the time it is off · put a question to *USER* that exists only because something
is off for now · grow a bridge into a second version of the part it stands in for · rebuild the missing part under
another name · leave a bridge standing once its part is on · call a thing proven while what the bridge stood in for was
the thing to be tested]
