# Do the cognition for them — the first law of copy

TRIGGER:[writing any copy a reader outside our context will read — a script, a page, an email, a post, an ad, an offer, a
pitch, an agent's outreach · striking or reviewing copy · a line that opens on "that", "this", "it" or "both" · a claim
with no "because" behind it]

PRINCIPLE:[the reader is not the one with the context we are talking about — the writer is · copy that leaves a gap is
written for an avatar already so versed in the subject that they "just get it", and that avatar is the writer · the ideal
reader is hypnotically inducted by the fact that the cognition was done for them]

DO:[name every term in full on the line that uses it — "whether or not you need X is no longer the question", never "that
is no longer the question" · chain every claim to its cause and its consequence with because · so · then · which means,
down to what it costs the reader, until the reasoning is complete on the page · break the subject down into its parts and
walk the reader through the breakdown — the decomposition IS the cognition, so give them the logic, never only its
conclusion · read every line cold before showing it, ask what a reader with none of our context would have to work out
there, and write that out]

NOT:[a "that", "this" or "it" whose referent the reader must recover · a claim with its reason left out · a conclusion
with its chain left out · a term swapped for a pointer to save words · a line trusted because it reads well to us · a
swear, in a hook, a title, a line or a CTA — the plain word carries the same meaning ("manifestation is nonsense", "then
do the thing")]
