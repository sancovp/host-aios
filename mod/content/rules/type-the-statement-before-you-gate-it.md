# Type the statement before you gate it (the types are the stations)

TRIGGER:[about to fire a gate (⊢|⊬) at any statement · about to write "the proof" / "the argument" / "therefore" / "it follows" of anything · about to grade a correspondence · reading a statement that closes on itself (the four-truths shape) · any "or else" / "otherwise" clause · about to call a self-answering statement self-granting]
DO:[TYPE IT FIRST — name its PLANE (REALITY: what exists, read or measured · VISION: the ruled future of reality · ABSTRACT: thinking, not yet a claim) and the STATION the statement is a move at, before any instrument touches it: 1 → (a brute fact · an ostension — the thing given a form; NO premises) · 3 A→1→2→3→B (an inference — the arrow OPENED into its derivation) · 6 F(x)=x (a closure — self-answering BY CONSTRUCTION: the four truths, every fixpoint) · 7 ⊢|⊬ (a verdict) · 12 ⌜X⌝ (a label) · fire a gate ONLY at a station-3 move · grade ONLY after typing (a brute fact has no grade) · say the type in words before saying anything else about it ("this is a closure, not an argument")]
NOT:[fire station 7 at a station-6 statement (self-ANSWERING ≠ self-GRANTING — "the existence of a fixpoint licenses nothing" cuts the other way too: a closure is not a self-grant) · call a brute fact "the proof" · coerce a statement into inferential shape because that shape is fluent · read "X, or else Y" as modus tollens when it is OSTENDING X · let the G-ladder stand in for typing (it grades CORRESPONDENCES, never STATEMENT-TYPES) · dress a brute fact as reconstructible (it makes the undefeasible look contestable)]
COMPOSES:[[[correspondence-grading]] — grade AFTER typing; a brute fact has no grade · [[28b-every-workflow-is-a-slice-of-one-sequence]] — the enum is the 13, not a minted list · "type before you gate" IS the ORDER gate (no step spends a later step's machinery), stated at statement grain]
