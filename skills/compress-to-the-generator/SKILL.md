# COMPRESS TO THE GENERATOR

⛔ **THE WRONG OPERATOR IS DELETION.** Cutting a column because it "is only provenance" removes the
reader's ability to answer *why this one* — and nothing in the shortened text reports that the
ability is gone. The text still reads complete. That is how a compression looks clean and is not.

## THE MOVE

**A list of N justifications of the same kind is a GENERATOR that was expanded and then written
down.** Compress by writing the generator instead:

```
why glyph₁ · why glyph₂ · … · why glyph₁₃     ⇒     HOW TO CHOOSE A GLYPH
N instances of one decision                    ⇒     the decision RULE
"in case A do X, in case B do Y, …"            ⇒     what distinguishes the cases
```

⇒ **the information is not removed, it is RE-SITED from the members into the rule.** A reader who
has the rule can regenerate any member; a reader who has the members can regenerate nothing.

## THE PROBE — this is the whole test, and it is behavioral

Ask a FRESH reader, holding only the compressed version:

> **"Tell me why THIS particular one."** (name a specific member)

| the answer | verdict | what to do |
|---|---|---|
| regenerates it, or something equivalent | the generator carries it | keep the compression |
| **outlandish** | the generator is too thin | **PUT MORE OF THE ORIGINAL INFO BACK** |
| refuses / says it cannot know | honest, but the generator is still too thin | restore, or state the member explicitly |

⛔ **THE TARGET IS BEHAVIORAL EQUIVALENCE: get the reader to the same place as if the info were in
it.** Not "the fact is technically recoverable." Not "a careful reader could infer it." The probe
either comes back right or it does not.

⛔ **THE READER MUST BE FRESH.** A window that watched the compression happen still holds the
originals and will pass every probe. That is measuring your own memory.

## DOING IT WITHOUT SPENDING THE TOKENS BACK

When a generator needs more than a sentence, the two instruments that carry generative power at
almost no cost:

- **ENCODINGS** — a notation, once paid for, states in one mark what prose restates every time.
  The paid mark is the compression; every later use is free.
- **DIAGRAMS** — a diagram encodes RELATIONS. Prose states relations one pair at a time and must
  restate each; a diagram states all of them at once and lets the reader read off pairs that were
  never written down. **That is generative power, not decoration.**

⇒ reach for these exactly when the generator would otherwise cost as much as the list it replaces.

## THE LADDER

Never compress in one step, and never judge loss at the end.

1. **v0 — FULL. No budget.** Everything known, in one place. Do not think about size here.
2. **Each next version names its COMPRESSION MOVE** (which enumeration became which generator).
3. **Probe against the level directly above** — loss is measured between ADJACENT levels, where the
   difference is small enough to actually see.
4. **Keep every level.** The ladder is the evidence; without the level above, the probe has nothing
   to check against.
5. Stop at the size, or at the level where a probe fails — whichever comes first.

⛔ A version that does not name its move and its measured loss is not a level, it is a rewrite.

## NOT

[delete a rationale column as "provenance" · assert a compression lost nothing · compress in one
step · probe with a reader that holds the original · keep members when the rule fits in fewer
tokens · state a rule so thin that no member follows from it · treat a smaller token count as the
achievement (the achievement is the probe passing at the smaller count)]
