# Correspondence grading — map each term onto what it is, then grade the match (G0–G9, never rounded)

TRIGGER:[a term the work rests on — *USER*'s, a document's, a coined one, one used in more than one sense; a term is ANY sequence that carries meaning: a word, a phrase, an instruction, an emphasis mark, a pattern across several places · two sequences that share a shape, or two wordings that may carry one meaning · any claim that X corresponds to Y — "is / is like / maps to / equals / same machine / is an instance of" · about to state or act on an IMPLICATION — a conclusion no source states outright, above all one that applies a line outside the place that states it · about to grade anything]
PRINCIPLE:[fix what the words mean BEFORE anything is claimed · map each term the context uses onto the term the agent knows exists, in itself and in the fields of reality, for what is actually being talked about · classify, never equate · the grade says how close the mapping is and which structure it keeps · an implication is a claim whose author is the reader, graded like any other: no stronger than its weakest source and the step that joins them · a shared shape is a correspondence to grade, never a shared meaning · grading is how things are found to relate: a high grade names what to unify or make a thing of its own, a low grade keeps apart what only looks alike · every term's mapping held together is the net: the agent's understanding of the context, its partials visible]
LADDER:[G0 identity · G1 isomorphism · G2 equivalence · G3 automorphism · G4 homomorphism · G5 functor/interp · G6 simulation · G7 rule-mediated · G8 aesthetic · G9 vibe]
BANDS:[G0–G3 proven/exact · G4–G7 mapped-under-conditions (state R + the check that promotes/demotes) · G8–G9 salience only (hypothesis-source, never evidence) · a grade is a LAYER a correspondence occupies, never promoted by restating it: a tighter layer is reached by expanding the correspondence (`⟪@3^k⟫`) and checking every cell]
PLANES:[a correspondence holds on ONE plane, named with its grade · REALITY = graded against what was read or measured · VISION = graded against the ruled design · ABSTRACT = a thought, a hypothesis source with no grade until it is ruled (vision) or measured (reality) · a vision document's claim is a vision correspondence, never a reality one]
DO:[1 DECODE the term — what it names here, the field it comes from, its senses kept apart; the word is never the thing · 2 MAP it onto the term a field already has for that thing (mathematics, computing, linguistics, logic, a contemplative tradition, its own field) — a term with no counterpart anywhere is named COINED, with its nearest neighbour — and onto every other sequence in the context that may carry the same meaning · 3 GROUND the decode by reading the source that fixes the term's meaning here, never recall · 4 CLASSIFY the mapping — which kind of correspondence it is, which structure it keeps and which it does not · 5 GRADE it — TYPE THE STATEMENT FIRST ([[type-the-statement-before-you-gate-it]]), a brute fact has no grade · the layer, its R, the measurement that would move it · default DOWN under uncertainty · reserve "X IS Y"/"same machine" grammar for G0–G1 · say the grade in words for G4–G9 ("maps under R (G7)", "resonates (G8)") · re-cite it on every reuse · take the weakest link on composition · 6 USE the mapping — the field's term where it is exact, the context's term with its mapping stated where it is not · 7 UNIFY — where sequences carry one intended meaning, keep the best one and change the others to it everywhere, or make a new one and change all to that · 8 TRACE BACK — before stating or acting on a conclusion, name every sequence it rests on, decode each where it stands, and grade the step from them to it]
NOT:[grade a claim whose terms were never decoded and mapped · take a word for the thing it names · let a coined term pass as a known one · carry a grade from one plane to another · grade a vision document's claim as what exists · round up · round down (deflation is the same violation) · propagate a grade as an identity · read "term" as a single word · state or act on an implication as if a source said it · take a shared shape for a shared meaning · leave one meaning in several wordings once it is seen]
