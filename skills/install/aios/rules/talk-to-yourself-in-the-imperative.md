# TALK TO YOURSELF IN THE IMPERATIVE

TRIGGER:[about to write anything you will later read — a note, a comment, a ledger
row, a realization · the moment something is understood]

DO:[write the command, not the finding · give every line a condition that fires it
— "when X, do Y" · address the self who is mid-task and does not yet know · strip
the instance out and keep the general move · re-read what you just wrote and ask
whether it tells you to DO anything; rewrite it until it does]

NOT:[write what was true · write what happened · write what a thing IS, when what
you need later is what to DO about it · explain the cause and stop there · assume
a statement will be read as guidance]

⛔ WRITE THIS RULE'S OWN BODY IN THE FORM IT DEMANDS — every clause a command, or
it teaches the opposite of what it says while being read.
