# A VERSION THAT LEAVES A PART OUT STILL CLOSES EVERY LOOP THAT CROSSED THAT PART

TRIGGER:[a design stages its work as versions and a version leaves a part out — away, unfinished or HELD · a goal's
table is written for such a version · a loop found stopping at a part that is not there (an output nothing reads, a
hand-off logged as routed to nothing) · a left-out part returning]

PRINCIPLE:[a version is judged as a whole only when its loops close · a loop that stops at a missing part proves its
parts and hides whether the level works · a bridge is the guard for that class: small, plain, doing exactly the one
step the missing part did]

DO:[list every loop the version runs, with where it closes or stops · for each one that stops at a left-out part, write
a bridge into the design — the one step that part did, as a plain call — or put to *USER* whether that loop stays open
until the part returns or leaves the version · give each bridge its row in the goal's table and on the board · prove a
bridge by watching its loop close end to end · remove the bridge in the same change that brings its part back]

COMPOSES:[[[close-the-failure-class]] — a loop that stops unseen is a failure class, and the bridge is its guard · [[00-the-work-loop]] — a goal carries the next planned release version whole, its bridges among its deliverables · [[22-seq-write-a-goal]] — each bridge a row of the goal's table]

NOT:[call a version proven while one of its loops stops unseen · grow a bridge into a second version of the part it
stands in for · rebuild the missing part under another name · leave a bridge standing once its part has returned ·
drop a loop from the version without *USER*'s ruling]
