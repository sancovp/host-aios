# THE THIRTEEN — the construction sequence, stated whole

The boot config names the 13 as the spine of the one workflow, and rule `28b` calls them THE GENERATOR of every
dev flow. This file states them whole: the stations, their glyphs, their symbols, the laws, and enough to
specialize them. It states the sequence so you can USE it; the derivation of why there are thirteen is not part
of this AIOS.

## THE THIRTEEN

Two columns, two notations for one thing, bridged: the **GLYPH** is the ADDRESS — what a seed, a board cell or a
verdict CITES — and the **SYMBOL** is the MOVE.

**TO CITE A STATION, WRITE ITS GLYPH.** `↥` cites the lift; `⊩` cites the gate. An example may only spend
symbols this table defines.

| # | glyph | symbol | the station | what it means, and what it refuses |
|:-:|:-:|---|---|---|
| 1 | `⟡` | `→` | representation | the thing given a FORM you can work. No premises; nothing is derived yet |
| 2 | `▣` | `A → B` | unopened morphism | ⛔ **NOT A MOVE — THE FAILURE.** Running 1..13 at ONE increment: a task chain wrapped in liturgy. It is listed because it is the thing to recognize, never a step to take |
| 3 | `⇶` | `A→1→2→3→B` | open morphism | PARTS, never a summary. The arrow opened into its derivation — a summary spent as the source is this station skipped |
| 4 | `⫇` | `⊆` | monotone refinement | `Info(Sₙ) ⊆ Info(Sₙ₊₁)` — each pass ADDS and never loses INFORMATION. Nothing it said or did is dropped on the way up; its text may be cut, merged or reworded (opened below) |
| 5 | `⩕` | `+1` | local witnesses | each part carries its OWN check, and **the verdict is never the runner's** |
| 6 | `≗` | `F(x) = x` | closure / LFP | the pass stops changing it — run up from empty until it stops. A closure is self-ANSWERING, which is not self-GRANTING |
| 7 | `⊩` | `⊢` or `⊬` (written `⊢`&#124;`⊬`) | admissibility | **THE GATE.** The existence of a fixpoint licenses nothing. A `⊬` is KEPT, never dressed as `⊢` |
| 8 | `⫤` | `⊨` | bijective reconstruction | it maps back, BOTH directions, nothing left over |
| 9 | `⩭` | `X ≅ X` | automorphism | same same but different. **ITS TEST, and what separates it from 8:** 8 asks *did it map back* — one round trip, nothing left over. 9 asks *is the thing that came back the SAME KIND as what went in, under a CHANGE* — swap an element for another of its type, re-run, and the structure must be unmoved. `⊬` at 9 = the round trip held for the instance you tried and breaks for a sibling |
| 10 | `⊚` | `E(you)=you` | reify | the operation becomes a CITIZEN of its domain |
| 11 | `↥` | `+1 → 1` | the lift — a **METASYSTEM TRANSITION** | what CLOSED here becomes one ELEMENT of the floor above: the closure coordinate crosses as the next floor's seed, and the old whole is the new part. `⊬` = nothing closed, so nothing can cross |
| 12 | `⌘` | `⌜X⌝` | new label | the name IS the diagram — and it is minted LAST, after the flow's own closure and gate |
| 13 | `♾` | `⊢ D∞` | re-entry — the global merge | never a step: the recognition that stations 1–12 factored ONE new entity. That entity re-enters as 1 — **the closure is the seed**. `⊬` = the 12 do not merge into one thing, so nothing re-enters |

## STATION 4, OPENED — MONOTONE COMPOSITION

**What is kept is information, never text.** A pass may delete, merge or reword text; it may not lose what the thing
said or did. Cutting text that carries nothing else loses nothing. Changing what a thing already does while adding to
it does: afterwards nobody can say which change produced what is seen, so the true behaviour is no longer known.

**In building, this is monotone composition: features do not subtract.** A new thing attaches through the interfaces
of what stands beneath it and never reaches under them to adjust it. That is why things stack, and why work done this
way keeps its momentum: it fits because what is beneath was made to be configured that way. Reaching beneath something
already released is allowed, and it has a price: what was reached into is tested again and released again before
anything stands on it.

**In wording, it is the least change that is effective.** Every added token can pull a reader somewhere unintended.
Sometimes the least change that is effective is all of it.

**Two things grow this way, and they are told apart.** What is built is STACKED: each thing runs on the one beneath,
and nothing beneath is modified. The builder is BOOTSTRAPPED: the agent doing the building is replaced by what it
built, which builds the next. A reader told only that a system is stacked does not infer that its builder is due to be
replaced, so say both.

## THE FOUR LAWS

1. **THE DEPENDENCY LAW — no step spends a later step's machinery.** This is the ORDER gate, and it is what makes
   the sequence a sequence rather than a list.
2. **⛔ THE ONLY ILLEGAL MOVE IS `▣`** — running 1..13 at one increment. A chain whose last station is a DOMAIN VERB
   (build it · ship it · fix it) has step 2 as its spine, and everything after it is inflation.
3. **THE DOMAIN IS THE MATERIAL, NEVER THE SPINE.** A domain FILLS the nouns of the 13; it never replaces a step and
   never adds one the sequence does not have.
4. **`⊑` — A STATION CAN BE OPENED INTO THIS CHAIN AGAIN AT ITS OWN GRAIN**, which is why one sequence serves every
   altitude. Its falsification: it fails at any station you can open into parts (station 3 applies) whose parts do
   NOT need the order — where a later part's machinery can be spent before an earlier one's with no defect. Find one
   and this law is `⊬` at that station, and the stacking is local rather than general. **State the grain when you
   claim it** — "you chose the wrong grain" absorbs every counterexample, and a claim nothing can convict is what
   stations 6 and 7 exist to refuse.

## THE STACK — chains at other grains, and HOW MUCH each one inherits

Each row is a chain at a chosen unit. **"Inherits" means it inherits the ORDER RELATION — the dependency law, no
link spending a later link's machinery — and NOT that its links map one-for-one onto the thirteen stations.** The
ALIGNABLE column says exactly which claim you can check from here.

|---|---|---|:-:|

| **SPECIALIZE** | question | Q1 what IS it → Q2 what is a PART of it → Q3 what is its GATE and what does `⊬` look like → Q4 what is it CALLED when it lands | **YES** — Q1→1 · Q2→3 · Q3→7 · Q4→10/12, stated below |
| **THE LIFT** | order | closure held ∧ every leaf witnessed ⇒ the coordinate crosses as the next floor's element | partly — it is stations 6 · 5 · 11, in that order |
| **THE TOWER** | floor | `D₀ —T₀→ D₁ —T₁→ D₂ ⋯` · ⛔ NON-STATIONARY: it does not terminate, it CLOSES — at **D∞, Scott's**: the bilimit of the tower whose rungs `Dₙ₊₁ = [Dₙ → Dₙ]` are joined by an embedding `e` and a projection `p` with `p∘e = id` and `e∘p ⊑ id`, a domain isomorphic to its own function space, `D∞ ≅ [D∞ → D∞]`. | **THE TYPE LADDER** | type | element → order → chain → sprocket → lens → chaintype → address | ⛔ no — `sprocket`, `lens`, `chaintype` are a notation this AIOS does NOT define |

| **THE DEPTH** | k | `3⁰ → 3¹ → 3² → 3³ → 3⁴` — work ordered at a stated depth is not done below it | ⛔ **no, and it is not the 13 at any grain** — it is a separate law about ordered depth |

## HOW A FLOW IS MADE — specialize, never invent

Answer SPECIALIZE's four questions in the domain's own nouns and **the phases fall out**: Q1 yields station 1, Q2
yields station 3, Q3 yields station 7, Q4 yields stations 10 and 12. A flow SELECTS the stations that apply; it does
not run all thirteen in order, and it never adds one.

⛔ **A DOMAIN WHOSE FOUR QUESTIONS CANNOT BE ANSWERED IS UNREAD.** It does not need a board or a flow; it needs the
read. Writing phases for it produces a fake sequence, which is worse than an empty row.

**STATION 12's "MINTED LAST" IS SCOPED TO THE STATIONS THE FLOW USES.** The label is minted after the flow's OWN
closure and its OWN gate, whichever of them the flow selected — never before them, and never on day one. `28b`'s
two worked flows reach 10/12 with one of them absent each (VOLUME PRODUCTION has the gate and no closure; CORPUS
ROLL-UP has closure and no gate), and both are correct. A flow that selected neither mints on the weakest available
ground: state that cost.
